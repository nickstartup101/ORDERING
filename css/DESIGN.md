## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the serene discipline of contemporary Nordic and Japanese café architecture: pristine white marble, hand-washed linen, polished light stone, and deep roasted botanical undertones. The interface rejects visual noise, excessive decoration, and hyper-saturated urgency, replacing them with contemplation, spatial proportion, and subtle materiality.

The target audience seeks deliberate culinary rituals, artisanal bean sourcing, and quiet luxury. The emotional atmosphere is tranquil, architectural, and tactile—evoking an early morning pour-over service in a sun-drenched Kyoto roastery.

Design aesthetic principles:
- **Refined Minimalism:** Uncompromising reduction where open air and balance serve as the primary visual structures.
- **Architectural Precision:** Razor-thin structural borders and subtle, calibrated micro-radii that lend a tailored, gallery-grade foundation.
- **Subtle Editorial Nuance:** Restrained, high-contrast serif accents anchored by a precise, understated geometric sans-serif core.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in elemental materials: warm chalk, pale travertine, roasted espresso, and oxidized forest leaves. Color is applied with deliberate restraint, reserved for hierarchy, selection, and tactile accents.

- **Primary (`#11241C`):** Deep Espresso Forest. The dominant structural anchor, applied to critical calls to action, high-contrast typographic focal points, and focused states.
- **Secondary (`#A39281`):** Warm Muted Taupe. Used for refined secondary metadata, tasting note highlights, active selection indicators, and delicate ornamental lines.
- **Tertiary (`#1A1A1A`):** Pure Charcoal Ink. Provides crisp high-legibility body text, subheadings, and precise functional icons.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF9F6`):** Light Stone. The foundational canvas surface that imparts tactile warmth while eliminating synthetic digital glare.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated surface cards, focal panels, and crisp interactive inputs to achieve effortless visual separation.
- **Hairline Border (`#E8E6E1`):** A razor-thin boundary tone used across dividers, card boundaries, and interactive outlines.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes the poise of editorial serif headlines with the pristine geometric neutrality of modern sans-serif functional text.

- **Editorial Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Deliberately restrained weights (Regular 400 and Medium 500) celebrate harvest origins, blend names, and curations without unnecessary heavy volume.
- **Interface, Body, & Microcopy (`DM Sans`):** Clean, open letterforms engineered for legibility across tasting specs, volumes, and transactional ordering pathways.
- **Labels & Categorization:** All label levels employ subtle uppercase styling with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to echo luxury horology and exhibition curation labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model balances generous, atmospheric negative space with strict grid alignment. Negative space functions as a deliberate design element, giving each composition breathing room.

- **Grid Discipline:**
  - **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column fluid stack with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding and `1rem` column gutters.
  - **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid, `1.75rem` margins, enabling side-by-side tasting panels and selections.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column grid capped at a maximum width of `1200px`, centered with generous stone margins (`2.5rem`).
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Generous vertical spacing (`space-xl`) between narrative sections creates an unhurried, calm browsing pace. Component interiors rely on compact padding (`space-sm` to `space-md`) framed by razor-thin bounding lines.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through delicate surface transitions and razor-thin outlines rather than heavy physical drop shadows.

- **Flat Surface Stratification:** Depth relies on the subtle tonal contrast between the Light Stone canvas (`#FAF9F6`) and elevated Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) card containers.
- **Hairline Framing:** Elevated cards and panels are defined by crisp, 1px borders (`#E8E6E1`), maintaining separation through graphic precision.
- **Ambient Micro-Diffusion:** When contextual elevation is essential (such as sticky headers, bottom order drawers, or flyout selectors), shadows remain soft, light-permeable, and deeply diffused:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 32px -4px rgba(17, 36, 28, 0.04), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(17, 36, 28, 0.02)`.
- **Frosted Translucency:** Floating bars utilize an airy frosted stone finish:
  `background: rgba(250, 249, 246, 0.88); backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces tailored, subtle geometric discipline. By selecting soft micro-roundedness (`1`), all components project understated architectural intention, avoiding bulbous or playful forms.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** Micro-rounded corners using `0.25rem` (4px) or `0.375rem` (6px) provide soft tactile edges while preserving sharp grid geometry.
- **Cards & Surface Tiles:** Contained with `0.5rem` (8px, `rounded-lg`) to create an unobtrusive, framed silhouette.
- **Imagery Containers:** Editorial photography of roasts, ceramics, and extraction processes conform to `0.5rem` boundaries or remain razor-sharp (`0px`) within bordered grid cells.
- **Pill Prohibition:** Full pill buttons (`rounded-full`) are strictly avoided to preserve the Scandinavian/Japanese gallery sensibility.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary CTA:** Deep Espresso Forest (`#11241C`) solid fill with Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) text, styled in uppercase `label-lg` with subtle letter spacing. Subtly rounded (`0.25rem`), minimum height of 48px, with interactive hover subtly lightening to `#1E382D`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent base framed by a 1px border (`#11241C` or `#E8E6E1`) with Charcoal Ink (`#1A1A1A`) text.
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** Understated text link featuring an offset 1px underline in Warm Taupe (`#A39281`), transitioning to Espresso Forest on hover.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Selection Chips:** Compact rectangular tags with `0.25rem` corners and a 1px border in `#E8E6E1`. Default state uses `#FAF9F6` background with `#1A1A1A` text.
- **Active State:** Inverts to solid Espresso Forest (`#11241C`) background with crisp white label text, omitting unnecessary check icons.

### Cards & Tasting Panels
- **Product Card:** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a 1px hairline border (`#E8E6E1`) with `0.5rem` rounded corners. Generous internal padding (`1.5rem`), featuring a 1:1 or 4:5 ratio image container, Playfair Display title, origin region in uppercase `label-sm` (`#A39281`), and a minimal price indicator.
- **Tasting Note Card:** Flat `#F2F0EB` stone surface with zero shadow, bordered by 1px hairline divider, presenting flavor notes (e.g., Bergamot, Jasmine, Honeycomb) in clean sans-serif typography.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Selection Indicators:** Square (checkbox) or circular (radio) controls with 1px border (`#A39281`), sized at 18px. Active states display a solid `#11241C` fill with a micro pure-white dot or minimalist checkmark.
- **Customization Matrix (Milk & Pour Options):** Presented as edge-to-edge bordered modular rows rather than loose form inputs, highlighting the active row with a hairline `#11241C` perimeter.

### Input Fields
- **Text & Quantity Inputs:** Crisp rectangular blocks with pure white fill, hairline outline (`#E8E6E1`), and `0.25rem` corners. Focus states swap the border cleanly to Espresso Forest (`#11241C`) with zero colored glow.
- **Stepper Control:** Segmented rectangular container with 1px interior dividers cleanly separating decrement, count value, and increment actions.

### Editorial Additions
- **Flavor Profile Axis:** A clean, 1px horizontal rule in `#E8E6E1` anchored by micro taupe crosshairs (`#A39281`) to chart roast depth and acidity.
- **Origin Specification Table:** Ultra-light tabular layout separated strictly by 1px horizontal lines (`#E8E6E1`), featuring alternating muted taupe metadata keys and charcoal values.
